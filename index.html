2. Decision criteria

Each criterion evaluates the complete original set of alternatives. Ties are all reported.

Assumptions: Maximax selects the greatest row maximum; Maximin selects the greatest row minimum; Minimax Regret minimizes the largest state regret; EMV/Bayes maximizes the probability-weighted payoff. Probabilities must total 1 (100%).

3. Staged filtering

Rules are applied in order to the preceding survivors. A tie at the best value survives; eliminated alternatives do not return.

4. Filtering flow diagram

Solid progression shows the alternatives that survive each stage. The final node is the recommended outcome of the sequence.
